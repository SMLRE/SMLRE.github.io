About

An engineer's instinct for systems.

I trained as a Mechatronics engineer and started out in instrumentation — calibrating sensors, reading signals, finding faults. Data science turned out to be the same job at a different scale: noisy signals, real systems, and decisions that depend on getting it right.

Today I'm a Senior Data Scientist in retail banking analytics at a Nigerian commercial bank. I own models end to end — scoping with the business, building the SQL feature layer, modelling, validating out-of-time, and deploying scores into customer-360 platforms that frontline teams use daily.

I also mentor junior data scientists and translate model output into decks for MD and ExCo audiences.

01Feature engineering at warehouse scale

Multi-table Oracle SQL pipelines, staging-table design for long-running jobs, feature stores with validation queries.

02Models built to be explained

Survival analysis, gradient boosting and SHAP — so risk teams know why a customer is flagged, not just that they are.

03Validation before celebration

Out-of-time tests, outcome-based weight validation, Gini against a logistic ceiling, threshold design for real operations.

04Insight that reaches the boardroom

Executive narratives, movement waterfalls and plain-language decks that turn analysis into decisions.

How I work

From raw tables to decisions.

01

Define the target honestly

Churn, default and attrition are business definitions first. I agree them with stakeholders before writing a line of SQL.

02

Engineer in the warehouse

Features live in SQL views close to the source — reproducible, auditable and cheap to refresh.

03

Model, then stress-test

Out-of-time validation, multicollinearity checks, calibration and SHAP before any number is shown.

04

Ship it where people work

Risk tiers and scores pushed into customer-360 views, plus a deck that explains what to do next.
